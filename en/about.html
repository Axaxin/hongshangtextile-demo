About Hongshang Textile

Founded in 2005, Foshan Hongshang Textile Co., Ltd. is located in Zhangcha, Foshan — China's premier knitting town. We are a large-scale modern textile enterprise integrating R&D, design, production and sales of fashionable knit fabrics. Our guiding principles are integrity, innovation and exploration.

Our dedicated R&D team releases new fabric designs every season. We support sample-based orders, OEM and ODM manufacturing, committed to delivering fashionable, high-quality fabrics at competitive prices.

Main product lines include: cotton-linen, dyed-woven, jacquard, rib, all-cotton, woolen, warp-knit and blended fabric series — widely used in mid-to-high-end fashion, casual and sportswear, serving globally recognized brands.
