About Hongshang Textile

Foshan Hongshang Textile Co., Ltd. is located in Zhangcha, Foshan — China's premier knitting town. We are a large-scale modern textile enterprise integrating R&D, design, production and sales of fashionable knit fabrics.

Our dedicated fabric R&D team develops new materials every season, committed to providing fashionable, trendy and competitively priced fabrics to customers worldwide.

Main product lines include: cotton-linen, dyed-woven, jacquard, rib, all-cotton, woolen, warp-knit and blended fabric series — widely used in mid-to-high-end fashion, casual and sportswear, serving globally recognized brands.
